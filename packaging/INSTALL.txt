Cat Pro-1 patch book (Studio 87)

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and open CatPro1-RecipeBook.html in your web browser.
Everything stays on your computer: no accounts, no network, nothing collected.

Files:
- CatPro1-RecipeBook.html  the recipe book: Pro-1 panel drawn at real proportions (also CAT, TD-3-MO, TD-3)
- CatPro1-PatchBook.html   the older simple patch book (works offline, patches included)
- patches/                 the same patches as separate .json files
- CatPro1-manual.pdf       the manual
- PATCH-FORMAT.md          the patch file format
- SHARING.md               how to share your patches

To remove: delete the folder.
